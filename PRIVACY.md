# Wordy: Privacy Policy

_Draft — review before publishing. Last updated: 2026-10-04._

Wordy ("the app") is a word game for iOS and Android made by Sargis Gevorgyan.

## What the app stores on your device

Your game progress, statistics, hint balance, language choice and settings are
stored only on your device. We don't run servers and don't receive this data.

## Advertising

The free version shows ads from **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect your
device's advertising identifier, approximate location from your IP address and
information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. On iOS, ads are
personalized only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change it
in Settings ▸ Privacy choices. See Google's policy:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

Players with **Remove Ads** or **Wordy Pro** don't see ads.

## Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google
Play). We never see your payment details. The app only learns which products
you own, to unlock them.

## Game Center

On iOS, if you sign in to Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's Game
Center leaderboards under your Game Center profile.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/SargisGevorgyan/WordleGame/issues>.
